Lesson assessment and optional transcripts — 12 November 2026

The course outline and class timeline offer separate Assess students and
Transcript & recap entry points. They open the corresponding panel in the
lesson feedback workspace.

Student assessment opens at the scheduled start of a non-cancelled lesson.
Teachers can save a private draft or explicitly publish manual observations
without a transcript, AI connection, or AI quota usage. A homework completion
selector records Completed, Partly completed, Not completed, or Not assigned;
leaving it untouched means Not recorded. Unassessed rating categories remain
null and display Not assessed, never zero or an invented score. Absence clears
star ratings. Homework completion is observational metadata, not a grade.

The optional transcript flow remains gated to the scheduled end of the lesson.
It retains its existing speaker attribution, transcript validation, AI quota,
supported quote/homework checks, and explicit publication. Transcript generation
does not require all rating categories to be filled. The screen states that
publishing the lesson report includes the current assessments.

Manual assessment publication preserves an existing recap and homework. When
there is no recap it leaves the summary blank and keeps planned homework intact.
Manual publication is retry-safe and revision checked. A concurrent AI job or
another teacher's change cannot be silently overwritten.

Published views retain the existing per-child projection. Students and parents
see only the linked child's assessment; parent notes remain parent-only. Teacher
drafts and transcript speaker labels are not projected to families. Drafts are
not made visible simply by saving.

The existing JSON storage accepts the optional homework status; no schema
migration is needed. Tests cover during-class boundaries, unavailable AI,
partial assessment, family privacy, preservation of an existing report, retries,
and the actual teacher UI on narrow screens.
