Live Board classroom flow — 12 November 2026

Insert live board opens the activity library, followed by a setup screen for the
title, instructions, visual layout, background, replies and reactions. The board
is created as a teacher draft. Supported shared layouts are Wall, Grid, Rows and
Timeline; structured activities also offer Columns and their graphic organizer.
Ranking activities retain their individual student columns.

Open to class publishes one shared board and places a reference page in each
enrolled student's notebook, in the matching section of the teacher notebook.
Custom sections are created if needed. A standalone board without a notebook
placement or lesson link goes into Notes. Teacher notes, files and assessments
on the source page are not copied. Existing student writing is never replaced.

Already-distributed references count as placed. Deterministic page IDs prevent
duplicates during retries or reopening. Deleted copies are not resurrected.
Protected sections are respected; a separate shared section is used if needed.
Students joining later receive published activities when their notebook opens
or polls. Legacy published boards gain placements when a teacher opens them.
Staff planning boards remain staff-only and do not create student pages.

The live classroom canvas is shared by teacher and student. Teacher controls
are in a cog drawer: posting state, appearance, layouts, teams, standards,
assessment, snapshots, archival and a retryable Sync student notebooks action.
Per-post moderation actions are in an ellipsis menu. Students cannot change
board settings or distribute boards. Existing class authorization remains in
force for board data, images, snapshots and contributions.

Notebook page, section and storage limits still apply. A partial delivery is
reported in the settings drawer and may be retried. Board delivery failures
do not prevent students opening or editing their other notebook work.

Validation: automated placement, retry, legacy migration, late enrolment,
permissions, private-content isolation, layout persistence and existing board
tests; fictional teacher/student browser walkthrough; desktop, tablet and
dark-mode visual checks. No actual classroom records were changed for testing.
